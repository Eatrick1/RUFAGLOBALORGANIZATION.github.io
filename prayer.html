Prayer Requests

You Are Not Alone

Let Us Pray With You

Whatever you are carrying — sickness, a broken relationship, a financial burden, a decision you cannot see clearly — you do not have to carry it alone. Our prayer team stands ready to intercede with you and for you.

Submit your request below and our pastoral team will personally pray over it. All requests are treated with confidentiality and care.

"Is anyone among you suffering? Let him pray. Is anyone cheerful? Let him sing praise. Is anyone among you sick? Let him call for the elders of the church, and let them pray over him."
James 5:13–14

Submit a Prayer Request

Fill in the form below. Our prayer team will lift your request before the Lord, and you may also be contacted for further pastoral support if needed.

Every request is read and prayed over personally by our team. We will never share your information without your permission.

What Happens Next

How We Pray With You

1
Your Request Is Received

Your prayer request goes directly to our pastoral and intercessory prayer team — no public posting, no exposure.

2
We Pray Over It

Our team intercedes for you specifically, often during our Friday prayer gathering and in personal devotion time.

3
We Follow Up

If you've shared contact details, a member of our team may reach out to encourage you and check in on your situation.
