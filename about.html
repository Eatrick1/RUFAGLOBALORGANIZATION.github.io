About Purified By Truth

Our Story

Built On Revelation, Rooted In Truth

Purified By Truth Ministries began with a conviction: that a generation grounded in the truth of God's Word, walking in the revelation of who Jesus Christ is, can carry Kingdom authority that transforms nations.

From our home in Luwero, Uganda, we gather as a people sanctified by God's Word, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and entrusted with the keys of the Kingdom — building disciples, equipping leaders, and strengthening the Church.

Vision

Where We're Going

To raise a generation purified by the truth of God's Word, established upon the revelation of Jesus Christ, equipped with Kingdom authority, and commissioned to transform nations for the glory of God.

Mission

What We Do

To proclaim God's truth, reveal Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior, disciple believers into spiritual maturity, equip them to exercise Kingdom authority, and build a victorious Church that advances God's Kingdom on earth.

"Sanctify them by Your truth. Your Word is truth."John 17:17

Core Pillars

What We Stand On

Truth

Uncompromising teaching and application of God's Word.

Revelation

Deeper knowledge of Christ through the Holy Spirit's illumination.

Church

Devoted to building strong believers and the Body of Christ.

Authority

Equipping believers to walk in Kingdom authority and purpose.

Meet The Founder

Sheila Nalweyiso

Sheila Nalweyiso founded Purified By Truth Ministries out of a deep calling to see a generation sanctified by God's Word and rooted in the revelation of Jesus Christ. Her ministry centers on teaching truth that transforms, leading believers into the revelation that builds an unshakeable Church, and raising leaders who carry Kingdom authority with integrity.

Under her leadership, the ministry is committed to proclaiming the Gospel, discipling believers into maturity, and advancing God's Kingdom across Luwero, Uganda and beyond.

Declaration

Our Ministry Declaration

We are Purified By Truth Ministries — a people sanctified by God's Word, grounded in the revelation of Jesus Christ, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and entrusted with the keys of the Kingdom. We believe the Church of Jesus Christ is victorious, that truth still transforms lives, and that every believer is called to walk in Kingdom authority, holiness, and purpose.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you meet?

We gather in Luwero, Uganda. Visit our Contact page for full directions and a map.

What are your service times?

Sundays at 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM, and midweek services on Mondays and Wednesdays at 5:00 PM.

Is there a place for first-time visitors?

Absolutely — every service is designed to welcome you. Reach out beforehand on our Contact page and our team will be ready for you.

How can I get involved?

From Bible study to outreach and youth ministry, there's a place for you. Contact us and we'll connect you with a ministry team.
